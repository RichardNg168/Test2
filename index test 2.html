Welcome to Yuki & White

Twenty years of quiet consistency.

Since 1999 we have provided office and commercial cleaning across Singapore. Over that time we have added the services our clients kept asking for — office renovation and fit-out, fogging and disinfecting, and a growing indoor plant business.

When you engage Yuki & White as your commercial cleaning company, the right measures are already in place to keep your workplace healthy and safe. Our team is selected carefully, because the promise is only worth what the person on your floor delivers.

Every visit

What a routine clean covers.

The same list, in the same order, every time — so nothing depends on who is rostered that day.

  • Trash, recycling and shredder bins emptied
  • High-touch surfaces disinfected
  • Floors swept, mopped and vacuumed
  • Washrooms washed, brushed and restocked
  • Workstations, desks and chairs dusted
  • Glass panels, doors and handles wiped
  • Pantry sink, counters and appliances cleaned
  • Phones, keyboards and handsets sanitised
  • Dispensers and supplies replenished
  • Indoor plants watered to schedule
